Max tilt →30: classy becomes seasseick. Perspective →200: the vanishing point moves closer; huge, dramatic 3D. Invert Y: the card now leans away from your cursor — that's the sign bug; it feels wrong before you can explain why. Transition off: loses all its weight and feels twitchy. Throttle off: recomputes on every pointermove instead of once per frame — same visuals, wasted work.
