# 00 — Vision & Positioning

## The bet

SkillJoy is a **"Stan Store for skills."** A creator gets one link
(`skilljoy.me/@username`), shares it on social, and sells their expertise from
it. The differentiator is **what** they sell: not a course, not a file, but a
**Skill** — one outcome-oriented package that can contain any mix of formats and
evolves over time.

## Who it's for

Non-technical social-media creators (TikTok / IG / YouTube) who have expertise
and an audience but no easy way to package and sell it. They are **not**
developers. The product must never expose infrastructure language ("API,"
"webhook," "Stripe dashboard," "bucket," "signed URL").

**The 10-minute bar:** a creator can build and launch a Skill in under 10
minutes, on a phone, without help. If they can't, the product fails. Every
design decision is subordinate to this.

## Why "Skill," not "product" or "course"

Incumbents force a choice up front: *are you making a course, or selling a
download?* That choice is friction and it boxes the creator in. SkillJoy removes
it. The creator makes **one thing — a Skill —** and drops in blocks:

- A "course" is a Skill with several video blocks.
- A "prompt pack" is a Skill with prompt blocks.
- A "template kit" is a Skill with file/workflow blocks.
- A "coaching offer" is a Skill with a coaching-link block.
- Most real offers are a *mix* — and that mix is the point.

This unification **is** the product. Keep it central in every screen and every
piece of copy.

## How it differs from Stan and other incumbents

| Axis | Incumbents | SkillJoy v3 |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Selling unit | course **or** download **or** call | one **Skill** = any mix |
| Updates | re-upload / new product | **versioning** — buyers auto-get v2 |
| Community | bolt-on or absent | lightweight space **per Skill**, buyer-gated |
| Analytics | "basic" (a named competitor weakness) | visual funnel + engagement + retention |
| Trust | silent freezes are common | **transparent payouts, human review, no silent freeze** |
| Aesthetic | often dated | modern 2026 polish (polish *is* the trust signal) |

## Positioning one-liner

*Sell courses, templates, workflows, prompts, and coaching as one Skill, from
one link.*

## Non-goals (positioning)

SkillJoy is **not** a marketplace. There is no discovery or browse-to-buy.
Creators bring their own traffic; SkillJoy is the storefront and the rails, not
the audience. (Full out-of-scope list in [doc 02](02-scope-in-and-out.md).)
